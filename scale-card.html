Sharkive scale card

Lay this card flat next to a shark or ray before you photograph it. In the app, tap the two ends of the black-and-white bar, then the two ends of the animal, and the app works out its length.

  1. Print at actual size (100%, "no scaling", not "fit to page").
  2. Check it: the bar must measure exactly 20 cm with a ruler. If not, the printer scaled it.
  3. Laminate it or put it in a plastic sleeve so it survives fish scales and sea water.

For the best estimate, put the card right beside the animal (not in front of it or behind it), and take the photo from far enough away that the whole animal is in the frame without tilting the phone.
